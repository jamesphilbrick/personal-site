# About This Site
## Purpose
I have multiple purposes for this website:

- **Personal reference** - it's a space in which I can collect, curate, and archive my experiences and easily share them with others. I'm more likely to reflect and appreciate trips, experiences, and life events if I take the time to write them up and subsequently force family and friends to read about them. 
- **Professional reference** - it's also a space in which to collate information and experiences related to my professional life which would very likely be too niche for other platforms. Other than that soul-devoid-corporate-platform-that-shall-not-be-named which rhymes with "BlinkedBin". Instead of forcing my family and friends to read about all the industry-specific skills I've gained and the projects I've worked on, I can instead shove it under the nose of (preferably deep-pocketed) prospective employees!
- **Unconstrained content sharing** - other social platforms such as Instagram or Facebook are necessarily limited in their format (usually some block of text attached to some set of images). Whereas this is my kingdom. For all intents and purposes, I can make it what I want it to be, without competing with some attention-centred algorithm or image count limitations or dopamine-inducing :material-heart: or :material-thumb-up: icons.
- **Its fun!**
