# Interesting Sites
Some interesting sites I've come across - give them a click if you feel like surfing the web old-style!

- [Curious Cones](https://cones.lol/) - "collection of cones that have found themselves in peculiar circumstances".
- [Photoloop](https://munnchai.itch.io/photoloop) - A fun casual game where wherein you're required to use nothing but the lasso tool to edit peoples' photos to get your rent paid on time. 

## Web Comics
- [Sarah Anderson Comics](https://www.instagram.com/sarahandersencomics/) - all sorts, with a simple yet one-of-a-kind art style.
- [xkcd](https://xkcd.com/) - frequently found in the slides of STEM university lecturers, and bound to satisfy your inner nerd.
